One poster that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026, on a light, airy
front page: three large hero scenes, eighteen compact stories, a numbers
strip, a large numbered world map with one dot per located headline, and
nine more angles in ALSO ON. 21 stories in all. Every story was verified
as a 19 November 2026 event (or first reported that day) before drawing;
disputed claims are attributed, not stated as fact.

- **Lead:** Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen — the first trilateral
  summit of the three, 90 minutes, one "peace declaration", and no big
  deal signed. Nuclear arms control, Ukraine and Iran on the table;
  Ukraine and its European allies were not in the room.
- **Gulf:** Iran widens its strikes to Kuwait and Bahrain, and a tanker
  hits a mine and burns in the Strait of Hormuz. Trump and Xi agree to
  keep Hormuz energy flowing.
- **Markets:** Anthropic's IPO raises $81.3 billion — the largest IPO in
  history — priced at $83 a share, 980 million shares sold. ANT opens
  on Nasdaq at $111.50, up 34%.
- **War and justice:** Ukraine hits the Kerch bridge a third time in
  four days; a Houthi missile damages Riyadh's PIF Tower; the ICJ rules
  9–6 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; Israel
  pushes into Gaza near Nuseirat despite the ceasefire; North Korea
  expands uranium enrichment at Yongbyon.
- **Politics and climate:** COP31 stalls on fossil-fuel wording;
  Denmark's parliament unanimously backs an Arctic defence pact with
  Greenland and the US; Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries as House
  Speaker, 251–11.
- **Sea, space, energy:** the LPG tanker Gas Husky sinks off Bulgaria
  with 3,400 tonnes of LPG aboard; NASA sets 29 November for a Falcon 9
  cargo launch to the ISS; the IEA says data-centre emissions rise 70%
  by 2030; a Yunus family home is torched in Dhaka.
- **New in v2 — Washington and beyond:** the Senate blocks the Iran
  war-powers vote 51–48 and passes a permitting bill 62–33; the IMF
  warns global debt is near 100% of GDP; Trump pardons Pete Rose
  posthumously; Kushner says Russia is "comfortable" with Article 5
  guarantees; the US leases 31.5 million Pacific acres for deep-sea
  mining, $6 million, a first of its kind.

Also on Thursday: Trump and Xi on Hormuz, Anthropic's $26 billion
private placement ($107.3 billion in all), the 76ers rallying from 17
down to beat Orlando, Griezmann named MLS Newcomer of the Year, NASA's
cargo ship named S.S. James 'Jim' A. Lovell, COP31's split over a
2035 electrification target, the DOJ dropping the Alaska Senate election
lawsuit, Trump saying no Iran war decision until the new year, and Ella
Langley sweeping the CMA Awards.

The image is drawn programmatically (Python + Pillow, Noto Sans) so
every word stays crisp — no AI-generated garbled text. Sources: AP,
Reuters, Wall Street Journal, New York Times, Just Security, CoinDesk,
NASA, IAEA, IEA.

## Entry

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is 1024×1024, 188,719 bytes (under 1 MiB).
Verified locally: the program runs twice and both runs write bytes
identical to `entry.png`.

## Validation

- Legibility checked at 300 px wide (phone size): the date, the three
  hero headlines, the numbers strip and the map stay readable.
- Numbers strip uses only verified figures: $81.3B, $111.50, 980M,
  9–6, 251–11, 70%, 51–48, 62–33.
- The map carries one dot per located headline (19 dots); the crowded
  Europe and Washington dots are staggered with leader lines to their
  true locations. The two global stories (IEA, IMF) carry no dot.
- Kit check is run with the bundle before submission.
